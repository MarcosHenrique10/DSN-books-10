Just then, Buford arrived backstage
at the festival with the Easter
Bunny he had found. “Wait
for your cue, fuzz ball,”
Buford said. “I’m going
to get a snow cone.”

“I’m trying to be
a maniacal leader,”
Dr. Doofenshmirtz
complained. “Not some

lame kiddie entertainer.
I’m out of here!” But before
he could leave, Agent P dropped
from the rafters to stop him!
