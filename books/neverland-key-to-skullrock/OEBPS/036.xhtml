Explore the World of Reading
The World of Reading line of early readers is designed to offer emerging
readers books that they will want to read featuring characters that excite
them. With this series, readers will become expert explorers in the world
of reading at a pace that is right for them.

•Simple text•Word repetition

•Simple storylines•Compound sentences•Contractions

Super Reader: Grade 1–Grade 3
•Paragraphs•Suspenseful storylines•Character development
