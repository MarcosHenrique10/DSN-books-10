“Smee! Stop ringing that blasted bell,” yells Hook.

“It wasn’t me, Cap’n. Honest! This ship seems to have a mind of its own.”

BANG! Hook and Smee hear a strange noise.

“Smee,” bellows Hook. “Find out what’s making all that racket!”
Mr. Smee looks around but he doesn’t see anything . . . or anyone.
They don’t know that Jake and his crew have snuck onto the ship and are
hiding behind the barrels.
