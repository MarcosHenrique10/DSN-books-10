Phineas, Ferb, and all their friends are
filled with holiday cheer. But when every
kid in Danville is placed on Santa’s naughty
list courtesy of Dr. Doofenshmirtz and his
invention the Naughty-inator, Phineas
knows that he has to save Christmas! Will
he be able to convince Santa’s elves that
Santa Claus  has to come to town? Or will
Christmas pass Danville by?
