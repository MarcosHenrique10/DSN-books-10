Back at home, Phineas and Ferb’s
 older sister, Candace, was worried.

Her crush, Jeremy, hadn’t called in
 four days. “I’m not expecting Prince
 Charming to bring me a glass slipper,”
 she said. “But a phone call would be
 nice.”

Just then, Candace’s phone beeped.
 It was Jeremy! He asked if she wanted
 to play video games with him.
