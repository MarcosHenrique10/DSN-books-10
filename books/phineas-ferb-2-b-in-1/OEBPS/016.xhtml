It was a dark and stormy night. Agent P quietly rappelled
into Dr. Doofenshmirtz’s lab.

“You should know better than to sneak up on a guy like
that!” Dr. Doofenshmirtz exclaimed. “Anyway, I was reading
this weird book and there is a very interesting chapter that I
think might interest you.”

When Agent P moved closer, Dr. Doofenshmirtz trapped him
with the book!
