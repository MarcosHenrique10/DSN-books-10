They built a few bubble washers, filled them with
soap, and set out to find the evil platypuses.

They weren’t hard to find. The clones were wrecking
Danville!

Ferb blasted a purple platypus with bubbles.
Phineas’s plan was working. There was just one problem:
the gang was outnumbered.

Then Phineas saw their only escape. “Everybody
retreat to that factory!” he yelled.
