"Ooh! A treasure map to birthday surprises!” says Smee.
“Doesn’t it sound delightful, Cap’n?”

“Bah! Why should a puny pirate have all the fun?” asks Hook.

“Oh, I’m sure the sea pups would be happy to have us join in the
fun if we ask them,” says Smee.

“Captain Hook would never ask!” says Hook. “But you could.”
