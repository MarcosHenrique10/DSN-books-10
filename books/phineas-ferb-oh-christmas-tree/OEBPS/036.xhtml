It’s Christmas Eve, and Phineas and Ferb have just learned
that Danville’s fifty-foot prize evergreen was destroyed in a
freak accident, which means no tree-lighting ceremony! So
the stepbrothers and their friends head into the mountains
to find a new tree. But little does the gang know that the
evil Dr. Doofenshmirtz is plotting to make
sure no Christmas festivities happen!
Will their trip be a success? Or
will this be the unmerriest
Christmas ever?
