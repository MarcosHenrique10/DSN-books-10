“I remember two landmarks on my own map,” Cubby says.

Cubby turns the fake map over and draws an outline of
Never Land. He draws a musical note to represent Pipe
Organ Plain, the first landmark he remembers. Then he
draws a banana for the second landmark, Monkey Jungle.

“Hook has to pass both these places on his way to
Thumbs-Up Rock—that’s where the treasure is!” Cubby
tells his friends.
