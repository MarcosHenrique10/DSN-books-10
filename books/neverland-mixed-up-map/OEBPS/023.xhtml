Cubby scoots up Cuckoo Rock. Jake and Izzy are right
behind him, and Skully flies up, too.

“Look!” Cubby calls. “It’s Skull Rock! I know that’s
on my map.”

But when Cubby looks at his map, Skull Rock is nowhere to
be found. That’s when he realizes that he wasn’t following
the map wrong—he was following the wrong map. It’s a fake!
And Jake knows just who switched the maps—Hook!
