Jake, Cubby, and Izzy are busy polishing the Golden Dinghy
when they hear Captain Hook’s cries in the distance.

“HELP! The Never Falls!” Hook yells.

In a flash, Izzy jumps into the dinghy with her friends and
sprinkles Pixie Dust on it.

“PIXIE DUST AWAY!” she calls, and the legendary
boat rises into the air!
