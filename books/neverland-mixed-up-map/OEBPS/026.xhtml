Cubby, Jake, Izzy, and Skully race down Cuckoo Rock,
through Pipe Organ Plain, and up a rocky trail. In the
distance, Cubby hears screeching high in the trees.

“That sounds like Monkey Jungle!” says Cubby.

“And that sounds like the monkeys took Cubby’s map!”
says Izzy, hearing Captain Hook arguing with the monkeys.
