Cubby is leading Jake, Izzy, and Skully through the woods
when, suddenly, they turn a corner and bump right into
Captain Hook.

“OOOOF!” Cubby says as his map flies from his hand.

Hook plucks the map from midair and puts his hands
behind his back.

“A thousand pardons, puny pirates,” Hook apologizes.
“I really must learn to watch where I’m going!”
