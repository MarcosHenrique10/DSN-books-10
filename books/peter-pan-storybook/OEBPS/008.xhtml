“We can fly!” cheered Wendy, John, and Michael as they
 rose into the air.

“There it is, Wendy! Second star to the right and straight
 on till morning!” Peter said.

Soon they were flying high above Never Land. The
 Darling children thought it was the most magical place! They
 saw Mermaid Lagoon, the Indian camp, and even the Jolly
 Roger—Captain Hook’s ship. It was just like Wendy’s stories.
