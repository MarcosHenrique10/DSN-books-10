As a result of that sword fight, Peter was also responsible
 for Captain Hook’s other enemy . . . a crocodile.

When Captain Hook’s hand fell into the open waters,
 the Crocodile had gobbled it up . . . and had been chasing
 the captain ever since. Luckily for Captain Hook, the
 Crocodile had also swallowed a clock, so whenever the
 captain heard a ticktock sound, he knew he had to run.
