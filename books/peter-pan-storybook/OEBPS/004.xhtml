On a clear and starry night in London, Wendy
 Darling told her brothers, John and Michael,
 stories about the adventures of Peter Pan, who lived
 in faraway Never Land. The boys loved hearing
 her tales about sword fights and mermaids and the
 pirate Captain Hook.

But they weren’t just stories. Wendy knew that
 Peter Pan was real because she had found his
 shadow and locked it in the Darling nursery.
