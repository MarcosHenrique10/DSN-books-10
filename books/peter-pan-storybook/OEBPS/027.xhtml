When Tinker Bell heard Hook’s evil plan, she escaped
 from the lantern and hurried back to the hideout to save
 Peter. Just as Peter was untying the bow, Tink flew in!

“Hi, Tink,” said Peter. “Look what Wendy left.” He
 held up the box.

Tinker Bell tried to pull the package away, but Peter
 wouldn’t let go. Tinker Bell pulled harder. . . . She knew
 the present was actually a bomb! Tinker Bell told Peter
 about Hook’s plans and together they rushed off to save
 the Darlings and the Lost Boys.
