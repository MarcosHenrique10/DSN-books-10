Hook couldn’t believe his eyes. Peter
 Pan was still alive! His plan had failed.

John, Michael, and the Lost Boys
 fought off the pirates as Peter drew his
 dagger and attacked the captain.

When Peter turned his back, Hook
 lunged at him with his sword. But the
 pirate lost his balance and fell into the
 water—where the Crocodile was waiting
 for him!

Captain Hook’s crew was forced to
 surrender.
