“Watch this, Wendy!” Peter said. He flew down
 and challenged Captain Hook to a sword fight. Hook
 fought Peter to the very edge of a cliff, and as Peter
 backed away . . . he was fighting in midair!

Captain Hook followed Peter off the edge, but he
 couldn’t fly! Hook quickly grabbed on to the cliffside
 with his hook. Then he heard a familiar sound . . .
ticktock, ticktock, ticktock. “I’ll get you for this, Pan!” he
 yelled before falling into the water.
