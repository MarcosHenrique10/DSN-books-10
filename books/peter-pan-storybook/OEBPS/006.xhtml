As Peter and Tink flew about the nursery, Wendy woke up.

“Peter Pan!” she exclaimed as she climbed out of bed. “I saved
 your shadow for you. It needs sewing.”

As Wendy sewed, she explained that it was her last night in
 the nursery. “I have to grow up tomorrow,” she told him. Peter
 quickly realized that if Wendy left the nursery, he wouldn’t be
 able to hear her stories anymore. He had to do something!
