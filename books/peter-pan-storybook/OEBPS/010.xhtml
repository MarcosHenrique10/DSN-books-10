On deck of the Jolly Roger, the evil Captain
 Hook was busy plotting with Mr. Smee. “Blast
 that Peter Pan! If I could only find his hideout,
 I’d trap him in his lair!” Hook said.

Captain Hook wanted nothing
 more than to destroy Peter Pan. It
 was because of a fierce sword
 fight with Peter that he had
 a hook where his hand
 should be.
