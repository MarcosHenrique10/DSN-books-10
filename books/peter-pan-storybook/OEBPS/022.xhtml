Meanwhile, at Peter Pan’s hideout, Wendy told stories to
 all of the boys. But she was starting to miss her parents back in
 London. She told the boys how great it was to have a mother.

This infuriated Peter Pan. “Go on. Go back,” he said. “But
 I’m warning you . . . you can never come back. Never.”

That didn’t stop Wendy and the boys. They left Peter
 behind and raced outside—where Captain Hook and his
 crew were waiting for them!
