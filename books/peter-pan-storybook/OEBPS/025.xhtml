The pirates brought the children back to the Jolly Roger and
 tied them up. Captain Hook gave them the option to either
 become part of his pirate crew or walk the plank.

But Wendy proudly shook her head. “Peter Pan will save us.”

“Peter Pan will save them,” Hook said, laughing with Smee.
 “You see, we left a present for Peter. Peter Pan will be blasted out
 of Never Land . . . forever,” Captain Hook told her.
